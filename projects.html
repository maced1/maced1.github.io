TuneTank 2.0

Jan 2026 – May 2026

Laboratory for Analytic Sciences (LAS), NC State

A pipeline that fine-tunes OpenAI's Whisper large-v3 speech recognition model with as little manual work as possible. I worked on it with a team of six for LAS.

  • Hyperparameter search with Optuna, successive halving, simulated annealing, and grid search, with pruning of bad trials.
  • LoRA (PEFT), layer freezing, and early stopping to cut GPU memory use and wasted compute.
  • Training loss, WER, and CER logged to Weights & Biases, plus a Streamlit dashboard for comparing runs and picking the best model.

  • PEFT
  • Optuna

GitHub Issue Auto-Labeler

Aug 2025 – Dec 2025

NC State, mentored by Rahul Pandita (GitHub)

A text classifier that labels GitHub issues as bug, enhancement, or question. Built by a team of three.

  • Pulled 803K issues from Google BigQuery with SQL.
  • Fine-tuned DistilBERT with LoRA on 15 TypeScript repositories and reached an F1 of 0.896, compared to 0.847 for zero-shot gpt-5-mini and 0.874 for human annotators.
  • Compared transformer models and GPU setups, which helped reduce GPU memory use by 44% with little loss in F1.
